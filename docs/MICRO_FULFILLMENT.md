# AuraMart Micro-Fulfillment Center (MFC) Guide (DARKSTORE-001)

## 1. MFC Architecture

Micro-Fulfillment Centers (MFCs / Darkstores) operate within high-density urban nodes (2,000 to 4,000 sq ft) holding top 3,000 fast-moving SKUs for instant fulfillment.

---

## 2. FEFO & Expiry Management

Perishable items use FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) shelf rotation policies enforced via barcode scanning during picking verification.
