# AuraMart Regional Localization Guide (CONTENT-010)

## 1. Overview

Regional Localization controls market-specific profiles across India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, and Oman.

---

## 2. Market Configuration Rules

- **India (`IN`):** INR currency, GST 18%, COD enabled, +91 phone prefix.
- **UAE (`AE`):** AED currency, VAT 5%, COD enabled, +971 phone prefix.
- **Saudi Arabia (`SA`):** USD currency, VAT 15%, COD enabled, +966 phone prefix.
