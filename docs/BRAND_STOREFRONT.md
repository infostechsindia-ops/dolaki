# AuraMart Brand Flagship Storefront Engine (CONTENT-007)

## 1. Overview

The Brand Storefront Engine (`web/src/app/brands/[slug]/page.tsx`) powers official brand mall experiences for major global & domestic brands (Apple, Samsung, Nike, Sony, Adidas, Noise, Boat, L'Oreal, Nestlé, Amul).

---

## 2. Brand Page Modules

- **Brand Story & Heritage:** Brand history timeline, mission statement, founder notes.
- **Official Brand Mall Badge:** Verified brand partner mark.
- **Brand Campaigns & Festival Sales:** Integrated campaign banners (e.g. Apple Week, Samsung Days, Nike Festival).
- **Follow Brand:** Instant product drop notification subscription.
