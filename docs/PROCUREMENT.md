# Supplier & Procurement Center Guide

## Overview

The **Supplier & Procurement Center** module ([`/operations/procurement`](file:///Users/arifalnukhbah/antigravity/AuraMart/admin/src/app/operations/procurement/page.tsx)) controls enterprise procurement, B2B supplier relationship management, Purchase Order (PO) workflows, Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), Quality Control (QC) inspections, and automated supplier performance scoring.

---

## Supplier Registry & Relationship Management

The Supplier Registry functions as the authoritative database for all B2B raw material and finished goods suppliers supplying AuraMart darkstores and central fulfillment centers.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  SUPPLIER RECORD: Apex Electronics Ltd (ID: SUP-10492) [TIER: PREFERRED]          |
+-------------------+--------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
| Profile & Tax ID  | Contracted Lead    | Payment Terms      | Active PO Count     |
| US-948201-X       | 4 Business Days    | Net-30 Days        | 3 Open POs          |
+-------------------+--------------------+--------------------+---------------------+
```

### Key Registry Attributes
- **Tax & Compliance Documents:** Tax identification numbers, ISO certifications, trade licenses, and environmental compliance audits.
- **Contract Terms:** Negotiated unit costs, lead times, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and payment terms (e.g., Net-15, Net-30, Net-60).
- **Fulfillment Centers Assigned:** Mapping to central warehouses (CWC) and regional darkstores.

## Purchase Order (PO) Lifecycle Architecture

The procurement lifecycle enforces multi-level approval hierarchies to safeguard operational cash flows:

```
[Draft PO Created] ---> [Approval Matrix Verification] ---> [Sent to Supplier]
                                  |
[Inbound QC Inspection] <--- [Goods Receipt Note (GRN)] <--- [Shipment Dispatch]
          |
          +---> Passed: Stock Injected into Inventory
          +---> Failed: RMA Issued & Supplier Debit Note Logged
```

### PO State Transitions

| PO Status | Description | Actionable Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `DRAFT` | Initial order creation by procurement manager or auto-generated by reorder engine | Submit for approval |
| `PENDING_APPROVAL` | Held for financial threshold authorization | Manager sign-off |
| `APPROVED` | Financial authorization confirmed; purchase order locked | Transmit to supplier EDI/email |
| `SENT_TO_SUPPLIER` | Supplier acknowledged PO and provided estimated shipment date | Await shipment arrival |
| `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED`| Partial shipment received at warehouse loading dock | Log initial GRN |
| `COMPLETED` | All ordered line items received, passed QC, and stocked | Trigger invoice matching |
| `CANCELLED` | PO voided due to supplier default or stock requirement changes | Release reserved funds |

### Financial Approval Thresholds
- **Level 1 (Procurement Specialist):** POs up to $\$5,000$.
- **Level 2 (Procurement Director):** POs up to $\$25,000$.
- **Level 3 (VP of Supply Chain / CFO):** POs exceeding $\$25,000$.

## Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Workflow

Upon physical truck arrival at an AuraMart fulfillment facility:

1. **Gate Entry Log:** Inbound vehicle details, bill of lading (BOL), and PO number verified at dock gate.
2. **Physical Unloading & Count Verification:** Warehouse team scans individual carton barcodes against PO line items.
3. **Variance Logging:**
   - **Shortage:** System logs partial GRN and flags supplier missing items.
   - **Overage:** Over-delivered units rejected or held pending procurement manager override.
   - **Transit Damage:** Immediate photographic evidence logged into GRN record.

## Quality Check (QC) & Inspection Protocol

All inbound goods undergo systematic QC inspection prior to bin placement:

### 1. Quarantine Placement
Inbound goods are moved to `QUARANTINE-ZONE-QC` and held unavailable for B2C/Darkstore sale.

### 2. AQL 2.5 Sampling Inspection
Inspections follow the Acceptable Quality Limit (AQL 2.5) standard based on lot size:

$$\text{Sample Size} = f(\text{Total Lot Size, Inspection Level II})$$

- **Visual & Packaging Defect Check:** Barcode legibility, seal integrity, retail box condition.
- **Expiry Date Verification:** Goods must possess minimum 75% remaining shelf-life upon receipt.
- **Functional / Electrical Check:** Random sample testing for electronics categories.

### 3. Inspection Outcomes
- **QC PASS:** Batch approved; system executes automatic transfer from `QUARANTINE` to `PICKABLE_BIN` locations.
- **QC FAIL:** Entire batch rejected; Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) generated, supplier debited.

## Supplier Performance Scoring Engine

AuraMart calculates a monthly composite **Supplier Performance Score (SPS)** from 0 to 100:

$$\text{SPS} = (w_1 \times \text{OTD}) + (w_2 \times \text{QPR}) + (w_3 \times \text{PCI}) + (w_4 \times \text{IA})$$

Where weights are defined as:
- $w_1 = 0.40$ (On-Time Delivery Rate - OTD)
- $w_2 = 0.35$ (Quality Pass Rate - QPR)
- $w_3 = 0.15$ (Price Competitiveness Index - PCI)
- $w_4 = 0.10$ (Invoice Accuracy - IA)

### Supplier Tiering Classification

| Performance Score | Tier Classification | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90 - 100** | **Preferred Supplier** | Auto-approval for POs under $\$25k$, priority payment terms (Net-15) |
| **75 - 89** | **Approved Supplier** | Standard PO approval workflow, Net-30 payment terms |
| **60 - 74** | **Conditional Supplier**| Mandatory 100% QC sampling, no auto-reorders |
| **Below 60** | **Blacklisted** | Supplier suspended from bidding; open POs cancelled |
