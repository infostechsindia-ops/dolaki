# AuraMart Social Commerce & Sharing Architecture (CONTENT-007)

## 1. Overview

The Social Commerce Module provides sharing capabilities, user collections, wishlist sharing, and influencer pick highlights across product pages, category pages, and seller storefronts.

---

## 2. Capabilities

- **One-Click Share:** Copy short link or share directly to WhatsApp, Twitter/X, Facebook, or Telegram.
- **Wishlist Sharing:** Public/shared URL parameters (`/cart?sharedItems=...`).
- **Influencer Picks & Curated Collections:** Curated collection resolvers embedded into SDUI homepage blocks.
