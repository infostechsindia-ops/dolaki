# AuraMart Category Landing Pages & Discovery Engine (CONTENT-004)

## 1. Overview

Category Landing Pages (PLP) provide a dynamic shopping layout across 24 master categories. Each category landing page is powered by backend layout endpoints (`GET /api/v1/sdui/category/:slug`).

---

## 2. Page Sections & Merchandising

- **Responsive Hero Banner:** Custom brand cover images, headline titles, and category badge taglines.
- **Subcategory Pills:** Interactive horizontal scroll pills (e.g. Smartphones, Laptops, Audio, Wearables for Electronics).
- **Official Brand Store Strips:** Verified brand flagships associated with the category.
- **Offer Strips & Promotional Banners:** Highlighted coupons, bank discounts, and Flado 10-Minute Grocery cross-links.
- **Faceted Catalog Grid:** Multi-attribute product listing with View Mode toggle (Grid View, Compact Grid, List View).
